## Overview

This project, supported by the Basic Public Welfare Research Programme of Zhejiang Province, focuses on future green and autonomous AIoT devices to achieve sustainable DNN reasoning in energy harvesting (EH) systems.

![Energy Harvesting AI Edge Computing Framework](assets/fidrl.png)

## Methodology and Results

Experimental results show that our approach improves the persistence and DNN inference quality of the energy harvesting system, achieving a 15.22% reduction in energy consumption for a single DNN inference, while comparing favourably with existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of accuracy and latency.

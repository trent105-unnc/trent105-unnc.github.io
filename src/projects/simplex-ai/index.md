## System Introduction

Simplex AI digital twin scheduling optimization system is based on the technological achievements of Ningbo Digital Port Key Laboratory, a deep integration of AI and digital twin technology to realize real-time mapping and intelligent decision-making of the whole production process.

## Core Technical Advantages

1. **All-element digital mapping**: Through high-precision 3D modeling and IoT data integration, achieve real-time twin mapping of factory equipment, transport vehicles (AGVs), and production processes, supporting second-level monitoring of production status and predictive maintenance.
2. **Intelligent Scheduling Engine**: Integrate a multi-algorithm fusion architecture, covering hyper-heuristic optimization algorithms, deep reinforcement learning models, and dynamic path planning technology.
3. **Cross-scenario generalization capability**: The algorithm itself can be generalized across multiple business scenarios, such as ports, factories, hospitals, and logistics.

The team has landed an AI-based container truck optimization transportation system at Ningbo Port, achieving an annual cost saving of 10 million yuan.

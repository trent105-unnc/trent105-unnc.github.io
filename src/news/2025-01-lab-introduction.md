The Digital Port Technologies Lab was initially established in December 2019 and subsequently awarded with Ningbo Municipal Key Lab (Class A) in 2023. The aims of the lab are to develop the cutting-edge technologies and systems for the next generation intelligent port, and to cultivate world class talent in AI and digitalisation.

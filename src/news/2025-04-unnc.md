This laboratory is established under the University of Nottingham Ningbo China (UNNC). As China's first Sino-foreign cooperative university approved by the Ministry of Education, UNNC fully incorporates the premium educational resources from the University of Nottingham in the UK. The University of Nottingham, a founding member of the Russell Group, consistently ranks among the world's top 100 universities. According to the ShanghaiRanking's Best China University Ranking 2026, UNNC ranks 65th nationally and 2nd among Sino-foreign universities.

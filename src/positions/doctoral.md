## Training Philosophy

The laboratory is committed to train talents with international vision, multi-faceted development, and keen perception of cutting-edge computer science knowledge and artificial intelligence technology.

## Degree & Duration

- Graduate PhD students will obtain a PhD degree from University of Nottingham UK.
- Full-time students graduate in 3-4 years.

## Application Timeline

Admission dates are in February and September, and scholarship application deadlines are in October and May.

## Requirements

Applicants are required to have a Master's degree with an average score above 65 (UK) or GPA 3.5 (US) or 85 (China) in the relevant subject, or an honours bachelor's degree with distinction.

## English Requirements

IELTS 6.5 with a minimum score of 6.0; TOEFL 87 with a minimum of 19 in Writing and Listening, 19 in Reading, 20 in Speaking; or a diploma from a university taught in English.

## Supervisors & Scholarships

Applicants will have at least 2 supervisors. Applicants can choose from 30+ scholarships that cover at least three years of tuition and a monthly stipend for living expenses.

The International Workshop on NeuroPhysiological Approaches for Interactive Information Retrieval (NeuroPhysIIR'25), co-located with ACM SIGIR CHIIR 2025 in Naarm/Melbourne, Australia, included 19 participants who discussed 12 statements addressing open challenges in neurophysiological interactive IR. The report summarizes the statements presented and the discussions held at the full-day workshop. Date: 27 March 2025. Website: https://neurophysiir.github.io/chiir2025/.

IEEE COMPSAC Workshops series is an established research event that complements the main focus of the IEEE COMPSAC conference, by focusing on specific topics that gain momentum in the research community. This paper introduces the overview of the IEEE COMPSAC 2022 co-located workshops, and discusses some open research issues that have emerged from the different workshops' topics and selected contributions.

# 宁波诺丁汉大学介绍 Introduction to UNNC

[← 返回总目录](../README.md)

本实验室依托宁波诺丁汉大学设立。宁波诺丁汉大学是中国第一所经教育部批准设立的中外合作大学，全面引进英国诺丁汉大学（University of Nottingham）的优质教育资源。英国诺丁汉大学是罗素大学集团的创始成员，常年位列全球高校百强及英国前 20 强。在最新科研卓越框架（REF）评估中，其计算机科学领域科研实力位居英国前列。宁波诺丁汉大学自 2004 年建校以来，已组建起一支具有国际视野的科研团队，核心成员均具备海外顶尖学术机构的教育背景和科研经验。根据 2026 年软科中国大学排名，宁波诺丁汉大学位列全国第 65 位，中国合作大学榜单排名第 2 位。

> This laboratory is established under the University of Nottingham Ningbo China (UNNC). As China's first Sino-foreign cooperative university approved by the Ministry of Education, UNNC fully incorporates the premium educational resources from the University of Nottingham in the UK. The University of Nottingham, a founding member of the Russell Group, consistently ranks among the world's top 100 universities and the UK's top 20 institutions. According to the latest Research Excellence Framework (REF) assessment, its Department of Computer Science demonstrates research strength in the top tier of UK academia. Since the establishment of the Ningbo campus in 2004, the university has formed an internationally oriented research team, with core members possessing academic credentials and research experience from world-renowned institutions. According to the ShanghaiRanking's Best China University Ranking 2026, UNNC ranks 65th nationally and 2nd among Sino-foreign universities.

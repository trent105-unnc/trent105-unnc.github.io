实验室在港口调度、数字孪生、安全生产、医疗、边缘计算等多个领域落地了代表性应用案例：

3. [Simplex AI 简优数字孪生优化系统](case-simplex-ai.md) — Simplex AI Digital Twin Optimization System
4. [基于工业互联网的安全生产数字孪生系统关键技术研究](case-safe-production-digital-twin.md) — Research on Key Technologies of Safe Production Digital Twin System
5. [楼宇智能应急仿真系统](case-building-emergency.md) — Intelligent Building Emergency Simulation System
6. [基于深度学习的剖宫产切口憩室超声图像自动分割技术研究](case-csd-segmentation.md) — Automatic Segmentation of Cesarean Scar Defect in Ultrasound Images

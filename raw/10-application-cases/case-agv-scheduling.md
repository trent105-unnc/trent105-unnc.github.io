## 项目概述

该项目依托第一医院复杂诊疗场景，研究基于数字孪生技术的 AGV（自动导引车）智能调度优化方法。

> This project focuses on the research of intelligent scheduling optimization methods for AGVs based on digital twin technology, leveraging the complex clinical scenarios of First Hospital.

## 研究内容

1. **数字孪生建模与场景仿真 Digital Twin Modeling and Scenario Simulation**：构建医院三维动态孪生模型，模拟门诊、药房、急诊等多场景的 AGV 运行环境，集成人流、物流及突发任务等随机变量。
   > Construct a three-dimensional dynamic twin model of the hospital to simulate AGV operation environments in outpatient clinics, pharmacies, emergency departments, integrating random variables such as human flow, logistics, and emergent tasks.
2. **动态调度算法开发 Dynamic Scheduling Algorithm Development**：基于 Anylogic 平台设计多目标优化算法，实现 AGV 路径规划、任务优先级分配及资源冲突消解，提升对临时加急任务和设备故障的响应能力。
   > Design multi-objective optimization algorithms on the Anylogic platform to achieve AGV path planning, task priority allocation, and resource conflict resolution.
3. **仿真验证与系统集成 Simulation Verification and System Integration**：通过虚拟现实交互界面验证调度策略有效性，开发孪生驱动的 AGV 智能调度系统，并在医院真实场景中开展示范应用。
   > Validate the effectiveness of scheduling strategies through VR interactive interfaces, develop a twin-driven intelligent AGV scheduling system, and conduct demonstration applications in real hospital scenarios.

## 意义

该研究可以推动医院物流管理智能化转型，优化医疗资源调配效率，降低运营成本，为智慧医院建设提供可复用的技术范式。

> This research will drive the intelligent transformation of hospital logistics management, optimize the efficiency of medical resource allocation, reduce operational costs, and provide a replicable technical paradigm for the construction of smart hospitals.
